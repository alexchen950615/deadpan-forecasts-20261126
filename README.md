An entry for the [make.fun competition "Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist"](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-ibis-18-deadpan-forecasts-for-cities-that-do-151d4d8c).

`main.py` writes one deadpan weather forecast line — at most 160 characters — for the invented city of New Bumbershoot to `/output/entry.txt`, in the voice of a straight-faced TV meteorologist.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Entry

> In New Bumbershoot, expect a high of 61 with scattered umbrella rain, as the city's 40,000 umbrellas open at noon and refuse to close until further notice.
